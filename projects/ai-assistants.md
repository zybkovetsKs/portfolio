# MintYachting — AI Super Yacht Concierge

**Role:** Independent solution design and hands-on delivery through KSENAI.

## Delivered solution

I designed and built a ChatGPT-based yacht concierge for MintYachting. The launched assistant helps prospective charter clients explore options and prepare for a conversation with a broker.

## What users can explore

- Yacht options and charter scenarios.
- Budgets, APA, seasonality and realistic pricing.
- Mediterranean and Caribbean itinerary ideas.
- Practical questions to discuss with a broker.

## My contribution

I translated the advisory need into a focused assistant, structured the expert knowledge, and built, tested and refined the experience through launch. This was independently delivered work, not management of a development team.

## Public evidence

KSENAI's service page describes the client project, links to the public assistant and includes commentary from yacht charter professionals. Their feedback emphasizes helping clients arrive better informed for a broker conversation.

The concrete result is a launched assistant for a defined advisory use case. No measured reduction in broker workload, conversion uplift or revenue increase is claimed. The case does not imply autonomous booking or replacement of professional advice.
